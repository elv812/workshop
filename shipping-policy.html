Shipping & Delivery Policy

Official fulfillment policy for ELV8 Academy online workshops, live executive cohorts, digital toolkits, and virtual masterclasses.

📅 Effective Date: November 2026 🔒 Certified: Razorpay & RBI Compliant 📍 Entity: ELV8 Academy (Chennai, India)

🚀 100% Electronic & Digital Fulfillment

No Physical Courier: ELV8 Academy delivers digital educational training and executive workshops. No physical shipments, books, or parcel couriers are dispatched.
Zero Shipping Charges: Shipping and delivery fee is INR 0.00 (Nil / Free).
Delivery Timeline: Workshop access credentials, Google Meet/Zoom links, and WhatsApp community invites are dispatched digitally within 2 to 24 hours of successful payment.

1 Mode of Delivery (Digital Channels)

All educational services and learning resources are fulfilled entirely through digital communication channels:

  • Email Dispatch: Payment receipts, welcome onboarding guides, and video conference join links (Zoom / Google Meet) are dispatched directly to your registered email inbox.
  • WhatsApp Cohort Group: Secure invite link to our moderated batch community group for announcements, live Q&A, and session updates.
  • Cloud Repository Download: Post-workshop presentation slides, workbook templates, recorded replays, and verifiable digital Certificates of Completion are hosted on secure cloud storage.

2 Delivery Schedule & Timelines

Fulfillment occurs according to the following strict timeline:

  • Immediate On-Screen Confirmation: Upon successful transaction completion via Razorpay, an instant registration confirmation is displayed on your screen.
  • Pre-Workshop Credentials (2 to 24 Hours): Meeting access links, syllabus guides, and WhatsApp cohort invites are dispatched via email and WhatsApp 2 to 24 hours prior to the scheduled workshop batch time.
  • Post-Session Collateral (24 to 48 Hours): Session recordings, presentation decks, practical toolkits, and verifiable digital Certificates of Completion are shared within 24 to 48 hours following session completion.

3 Shipping & Handling Fees

Shipping charges for all ELV8 Academy digital programs are INR 0.00 (Nil / Free). There are no postal, handling, packaging, or courier fees charged under any circumstances.

4 Support for Non-Receipt of Credentials

If you have registered and completed payment but have not received your workshop joining credentials or WhatsApp community invite within 24 hours of payment:

  • Please inspect your email's Spam, Junk, or Promotions folders.
  • If still not located, contact our support desk at fazeela@elv8.co.in or send a WhatsApp message with your Razorpay Payment ID.
  • Our coordinator will verify your enrollment and manually reissue credentials within 2 to 4 business hours.
